Biography

Oluwaseyi is an Associate at SimmonsCooper Partners, Lagos, Nigeria. Oluwaseyi brings a wealth of knowledge and dedication to the table. Her expertise spans corporate, commercial, finance, energy, legislative instruments, and policy.

Oluwaseyi’s commitment to legal excellence has earned her several prestigious awards and recognitions. Notable among them are the Hon. Justice Mohammed Bello G.C.O.N. Prize for the Second-Best Student of the Year at the Nigerian Law School 2020/2021, the Dr. Nabo Graham-Douglas S.A.N. Prize for the Second-Best Student in Corporate Law Practice, and the Chief Debo Akande S.A.N. Prize for the Second-Best Student in Criminal Litigation during the same period.
