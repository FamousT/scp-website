Biography

Victoria is a Partner at SimmonsCooper Partners. A licensed tax practitioner and Notary Public in Nigeria, her journey with the firm is marked by excellence.

Victoria’s versatility and in-depth knowledge in areas like Dispute Resolution, Alternative Dispute Resolution, Arbitration, Taxation, Public Sector Regulation, and Real Estate enable her to provide comprehensive and strategic counsel to clients. With over 33 years of practice, she leverages her vast experience to offer solutions and sound advice, navigating clients successfully through complex legal landscapes.

Education and admissions

  • Bachelor of Law Degree from the University of Lagos
  • Admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 1990
  • Master of Law degree from the University of Lagos
  • Joined SimmonsCooper Partners in 2008 as Head of Practice
  • Appointed Partner in 2015

Professional memberships

  • Nigerian Bar Association (NBA)
  • Fellow, Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (FCTI)
  • Associate Member, Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK) (ACIArb)
  • Associate Member, Business Recovery & Insolvency Practitioners of Nigeria (BRIPAN)
