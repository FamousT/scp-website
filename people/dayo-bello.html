Biography

Dayo is a Senior Associate at SimmonsCooper Partners and leads the Labour and Employment Team. His expertise spans several key legal areas, including Labour & Employment, Taxation, Arbitration, Construction, and Family and Wealth Management. Dayo’s broad skillset enables him to offer comprehensive and tailored legal advice to clients across these diverse fields.

He has played a significant role in academia as the Lead Legal Researcher for the School of Social and Political Sciences at the University of Edinburgh. In 2023, he was integral to the “Building Peace and Expanding Access to Legal Representation in Nigeria’s Gangscape Project” (the “Peace-Law Project”).

Dayo’s journey with SimmonsCooper Partners began in February 2023, marking a significant addition to the firm’s range of expertise and services.
