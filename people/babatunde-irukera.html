Biography

Mr. Babatunde Irukera is a lawyer and regulatory specialist with over 35 years’ experience spanning commercial/civil litigation, criminal defence and prosecution, international legal practice, institutional reform, market regulation, and public interest litigation in Nigeria and internationally. His experience spans legal practice, policy and institutional leadership, gained over decades of service in the public and private sectors, in Nigeria and internationally.

He is the immediate past Executive Vice Chairman/CEO of the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), where he led the transformation of Nigeria’s consumer protection landscape and pioneered the country’s first comprehensive competition law regulatory regime. Under his leadership, the FCCPC became a model for regulatory innovation, accountability, and market fairness. Currently, he is the Chairman of Veritas Kapital Assurance PLC.

Babatunde has advised and represented the Federal and State Governments, regulatory agencies, and international organisations in complex, high-impact matters including major aviation accident claims, and multi-jurisdictional consumer and competition cases. He has also been instrumental in shaping national legislation and regulatory frameworks in other countries in consumer protection, aviation, digital trade, and competition law.

A former Managing Partner at SimmonsCooper Partners, and currently Of Counsel, his work spans competition/commercial law, securities litigation, corporate governance, government relations and stakeholder engagement. He is also actively engaged in Africa-wide regulatory development, advising regional bodies such as ECOWAS, COMESA, and AFCFTA on key aspects of competition regulation.

Admitted to the Bar in Nigeria and in the United States, in 1990, and 1996 respectively, he has held different leadership positions in both the Nigerian and American Bar Associations. His contributions to law and regulatory reform have earned him several recognitions, including citations in Who’s Who Legal, a publication profiling leading lawyers in the United States, The American Lawyer and many other reputed legal practice or professional publications.

A commercial law firm providing holistic legal support for the Nigerian market and beyond, from offices in Lagos and Abuja.
