Biography

Ritason is a Senior Associate at SimmonsCooper Partners, where he is a member of the Firm’s Corporate Commercial and General Transactions, Antitrust & Competition Compliance, Legislative Instruments & Policy, and Commercial & Civil Litigation practice areas. He also leads the Firm’s Consumer Credit and Digital Money Lending practice team.

His practice spans a broad range of sectors and projects, with a focus on providing strategic legal and regulatory advice to clients on complex commercial matters, competition and consumer protection issues, legislative and policy developments, and dispute resolution. He brings a commercially focused approach to advising clients on navigating regulatory frameworks, managing legal risks, and achieving their business objectives.

Ritason joined SimmonsCooper Partners in Abuja in February 2026.
