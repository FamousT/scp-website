Biography

Oluwadara is a Senior Associate at SimmonsCooper Partners, where she plays a key role in legal strategy and business risk management. Oluwadara specializes in corporate, commercial, and competition law. Her legal practice covers a wide range of fields including Corporate, Commercial & Finance, Anti-trust & Competition Compliance, Energy, Projects & Transport, Family & Wealth Management, and Legislative Instruments & Policy. This diversity enables her to offer tailored and strategic advice to clients, addressing their specific needs.

Oluwadara’s approach to legal practice is marked by innovation and a forward-thinking mindset. She leverages her experience to provide clients with advanced legal solutions, adapting to the dynamic nature of the legal environment to help ensure their success.

As a member of the Management Committee, she contributes to policy development and oversees legal and business matters.

Education and admissions

  • LLM in Competition Law with Distinction, Queen Mary University of London (August 2020)
  • Admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 2016
