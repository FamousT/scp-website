Biography

Adegoke is a Partner at SimmonsCooper Partners and leads the Alternative Dispute Resolution team. His expertise spans Project Finance, Public Finance, Labour and Employment, making him a valuable asset to the firm and its clients. Adegoke’s vast knowledge and experience enable him to provide strategic guidance and innovative legal solutions, contributing significantly to the growth and development of SimmonsCooper Partners. His career encompasses work in Telecommunications, Maritime, and Shipping industries.

Adegoke has authored legal publications including “Project Finance: Identification, Allocation and Mitigation of Risk Factors” and “Arbitral awards in International commercial arbitration: Appropriateness of review by national courts.”

Education and admissions

  • Bachelor of Law Degree from Igbinedion University
  • Master of Law degree from the University of Hull, UK
  • Admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 2008
