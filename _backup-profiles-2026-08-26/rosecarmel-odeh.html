Biography

Rosecarmel is an Associate at the firm, working across the Commercial & Civil Litigation, Legislative Instruments and Policy, and Projects and Transport practice areas. She has published legal resources on several platforms.

Rosecarmel graduated with a First Class Bachelor of Laws from Benue State University in 2021 and earned her Barrister-at-Law at the Nigerian Law School in 2022. Since joining SimmonsCooper Partners in January 2023 she has exhibited promising legal talent and an eagerness to take on challenges.
