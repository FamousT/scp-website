Tech & AI

Data protection, platform regulation and AI governance.

We advise technology businesses on the rules that now govern how they build — the Nigeria Data Protection Act, NITDA guidance, sector licensing, content and platform liability, and the emerging expectations around automated decision-making.

For AI specifically, we help clients document model provenance, allocate risk in vendor contracts, and put governance in place before a deployment becomes a regulatory or reputational problem.

What we do

  • NDPA compliance programmes, DPIAs and audit filings
  • Cross-border data transfer and localisation strategy
  • AI governance frameworks and model risk allocation
  • Platform terms, content liability and intermediary duties
  • Technology contracting, licensing and IP protection
