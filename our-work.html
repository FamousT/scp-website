Energy

Financing structure for a multi-asset gas-to-power project

What it was
A sponsor consortium developing a multi-asset gas-to-power project across three states needed a financing structure that satisfied international lenders while surviving Nigerian regulatory review.
Why it mattered
Gas-to-power projects fail at the interface between the security package lenders want and the consents the state will grant. Getting that interface right determined whether the project reached financial close at all.
Our role
We advised the sponsors on the security package, host-community development trust, regulatory consents across three states, and the inter-creditor arrangements. We ran the consent workstream in parallel with the financing so approvals landed before the long-stop date.

Dispute Resolution / Public Impact Litigation

Defence of a multinational in a landmark commercial dispute

Outcome

Claim dismissed

What it was
A multinational faced a claim that threatened both its operating licence and its position in the Nigerian market.
Why it mattered
The case turned on a point of statutory interpretation that had not been settled at appellate level. The outcome set the reading for every operator in the sector.
Our role

Tech & AI

Licensing pathway for a pan-African payments platform

Outcome

First-of-its-kind

What it was
A payments platform operating across several African markets needed a Nigerian entry structure that would not compromise its existing licences elsewhere.
Why it mattered
No comparable structure had been approved. The engagement required the regulator to accept a model it had not seen before.
Our role
We structured the entity, built the data-protection posture, and led the CBN engagement strategy through to approval — the first authorisation of its kind for a cross-border platform of this design.

Competition

Unconditional clearance for a consolidating manufacturer

Outcome

Cleared in Phase 1

What it was
Two manufacturers with overlapping product lines sought clearance for a combination that would have created the largest player in its segment.
Why it mattered
On a first reading the transaction looked like a Phase 2 candidate. A Phase 2 review would have pushed completion past the sellers' deadline and put the deal at risk.
Our role
We built the economic case on market definition, engaged the Commission early on the theory of harm, and secured unconditional clearance at Phase 1 within the transaction timetable.

Corporate Restructuring

Consensual workout for an over-leveraged industrial group

Outcome

Nine lenders aligned

What it was
An industrial group with facilities across nine lenders faced simultaneous enforcement action.
Why it mattered
Enforcement would have broken up an operating business that was viable once its balance sheet was repaired. A consensual outcome preserved several thousand jobs.
Our role
We acted for the group in negotiating a standstill, then a scheme of arrangement that rescheduled the debt and released new working capital. We advised the board throughout on director duties during the period of distress.

Tax

Appeal setting aside a multi-year revenue assessment

Outcome

Assessment set aside

What it was
A client received an assessment covering several years, built on a transfer pricing methodology the client disputed.
Why it mattered
The methodology, if upheld, would have applied to a broad class of similarly structured groups.
Our role
We ran the objection and the appeal to the Tax Appeal Tribunal, where the assessment was set aside in full. The decision is now cited on the treatment of intra-group service charges.
