Public Governance & Tax Advisory Services

Resolution of Multi-Year Personal Income Tax Dispute for a High-Net-Worth Individual

Outcome
Substantial reduction of the client’s assessed tax exposure and resolution of liabilities spanning approximately seven assessment years on terms satisfactory to the client.
What it was
We represented a high-net-worth individual in connection with disputed personal income tax assessments issued by the Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS), involving alleged liabilities running into hundreds of millions of naira and spanning approximately seven assessment years.
Why it mattered
The engagement involved significant historical tax exposure across multiple assessment periods and required a detailed reconciliation of the client’s actual tax position against the assessments raised by the tax authority. Through the engagement, the client’s assessed exposure was reduced from hundreds of millions of naira to a single-digit million-naira liability. The resolution also regularised the client’s tax position and provided certainty regarding his outstanding tax obligations.
Our role
We reviewed the assessments and underlying tax position, developed the basis for challenging and reconciling the disputed liabilities, engaged extensively with the LIRS on the client’s behalf, and negotiated a substantial reduction of the assessed exposure to an agreed and satisfactory liability. The outcome strengthened the client’s confidence in our tax practice and led to our continued engagement on subsequent tax matters.

Public Governance & Tax Advisory Services

Capacity Development for a Leading State Internal Revenue Service

Outcome
Successfully delivered two specialised capacity-development programmes for one of Nigeria’s leading Internal Revenue Services, with exceptional commendation from the institution.
What it was
We were engaged by a leading State Internal Revenue Service to deliver specialised training and capacity development for its personnel on key legal, regulatory and practical issues relevant to contemporary tax administration and enforcement.
Why it mattered
Effective revenue administration increasingly requires tax authorities to combine technical knowledge of evolving tax legislation with sound governance, compliance and enforcement practices. The engagement provided an opportunity to contribute directly to strengthening institutional capacity within one of Nigeria’s foremost revenue authorities.
Our role
We designed and delivered tailored training programmes addressing the institution’s identified needs, combining legal and regulatory analysis with practical tax administration considerations. Following the exceptional feedback received on the first engagement, we were subsequently engaged to deliver a further programme, which also received outstanding commendation.

Public Governance & Tax Advisory Services

Tax Advisory on Corporate Restructuring and M&A Transaction

Outcome
Successfully advised on the tax-efficient implementation of the proposed restructuring and transaction, including the application of available Capital Gains Tax reliefs/exemptions.
What it was
We advised a corporate client on the tax implications arising from a restructuring and merger and acquisition transaction, with particular emphasis on the Capital Gains Tax consequences of the proposed structure and the availability of applicable statutory reliefs and exemptions.
Why it mattered
The tax treatment was material to the commercial viability and implementation of the transaction. The engagement required the transaction structure to be considered against the applicable tax framework to identify potential exposures, determine the availability of reliefs and ensure that the restructuring could proceed without avoidable tax costs.
Our role
We analysed the proposed transaction structure and its tax consequences, advised on the applicability of Capital Gains Tax and available statutory reliefs/exemptions, and provided guidance on structuring and implementing the transaction in a tax-efficient and compliant manner.
