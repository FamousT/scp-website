Abuja — Federal Capital Territory

Regulatory work at the seat of government.

Our Abuja office exists for the moments when outcomes turn on regulators, not courtrooms. We secure licences, negotiate concessions and make policy submissions before the ministries, commissions and agencies that govern regulated industries.

What the Abuja desk does

Regulated business in Nigeria is decided in Abuja long before it reaches a courtroom. Licences, concessions, tariff approvals and sector rules are settled through engagement with ministries, commissions and agencies — and that engagement has its own timetable, its own evidence standards and its own politics.

Our Abuja practice runs that engagement for clients: preparing applications that survive scrutiny, making submissions during rule-making, and keeping a live picture of what each regulator is about to do next.
