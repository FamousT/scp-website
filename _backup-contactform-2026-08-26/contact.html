Media enquiries.

Journalists and researchers should use the enquiry form, selecting Press / media as the topic. Requests reach the Marketing team, who will arrange comment, background or an interview with the relevant partner.

We aim to respond to media enquiries the same working day. For deadlines inside two hours, note the deadline in your message and call the Lagos switchboard after submitting.
