Restructuring

Corporate Restructuring

Balance-sheet repair, insolvency and distressed M&A.

We act for companies, creditors and investors in restructurings under CAMA 2020 — schemes of arrangement, company voluntary arrangements, administration and the negotiated workouts that avoid all three.

Our restructuring work runs alongside our finance and litigation practices, so security enforcement, director duties and stakeholder litigation are managed as one strategy rather than three.

What we do

  • Schemes of arrangement and creditor negotiations
  • Administration, receivership and winding-up
  • Distressed M&A and pre-pack disposals
  • Director duties, wrongful trading and board advice
  • Security enforcement and inter-creditor arrangements
