Competition Law

Merger control, market conduct and FCCPC engagement.

We advise on the full span of Nigerian and regional competition law — from notifying a transaction to the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, through to defending conduct investigations and building compliance programmes that hold up under scrutiny.

Our work sits at the point where commercial strategy meets regulatory tolerance. We help boards understand what a deal or a pricing decision looks like from the regulator's side of the table, and we structure engagement so that approvals arrive on the timetable the transaction actually needs.

What we do

  • Merger notification, Phase 1 and Phase 2 review, and remedy negotiation
  • Abuse of dominance and restrictive-agreement investigations
  • Competition compliance audits, policies and board training
  • Market studies, sector inquiries and policy submissions
  • Distribution, franchise and exclusivity arrangements
