Competition

Competition Law

Operating within the competitive Nigerian market requires businesses to be vigilant and compliant with competition and antitrust laws, which are complex and multifaceted.

We advise on Competition Law & Antitrust and Merger Review. As a consultant to the World Bank Group, the firm contributed to the development of Nigeria’s competition law framework, which encompasses merger review, restrictive agreements, abuse of dominance, and market definition.

We advise domestic and international businesses on competition matters, including merger control. Beyond legal representation, we also facilitate capacity building, offering seminars and workshops to elucidate the nuances of competition law compliance. These cover the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) and the regulations made under it, so that clients can comply and avoid penalties.

What we do

  • Merger notification, Phase 1 and Phase 2 review, and remedy negotiation
  • Abuse of dominance and restrictive-agreement investigations
  • Competition compliance audits, policies and board training
  • Market studies, sector inquiries and policy submissions
  • Distribution, franchise and exclusivity arrangements
