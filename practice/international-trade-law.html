International Trade Law

Businesses engaging in international commerce require legal guidance that is both expert and nuanced.

Our International Trade practice recognises the critical importance of cross-border trade in today’s interconnected global economy. With a multitude of regulations and policies to consider, businesses engaging in international commerce require legal guidance that is both expert and nuanced.

We advise on trade agreements, customs law, export controls, and ESG considerations within international trade contexts. We help clients comply with current trade rules and prepare for changes to them, including on trade remedies, unfair competition and ESG requirements in international operations.

We also act in the sphere of rulemaking and policy development, offering legislative drafting, policy analysis, and strategic advice on public policy engagement. Our team, equipped with a deep understanding of governmental processes, assists organisations in interpreting policy implications and engaging in informed advocacy, serving as a bridge between the business community and regulatory bodies.

What we do

  • AfCFTA rules of origin and preferential-tariff strategy
  • Customs classification, valuation and dispute resolution
  • Anti-dumping, countervailing and safeguard investigations
  • Export controls, sanctions and trade compliance
  • Investment treaty protection and market access
