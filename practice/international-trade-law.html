Trade

International Trade Law

AfCFTA, customs, trade remedies and market access.

We advise exporters, importers and governments on the rules that govern goods and services crossing African borders — AfCFTA rules of origin, tariff classification, customs valuation and the trade-remedy investigations that follow a surge in imports.

We also support investors on market-access strategy, treaty protection and the practical business of moving product through Nigerian ports without avoidable delay.

What we do

  • AfCFTA rules of origin and preferential-tariff strategy
  • Customs classification, valuation and dispute resolution
  • Anti-dumping, countervailing and safeguard investigations
  • Export controls, sanctions and trade compliance
  • Investment treaty protection and market access
