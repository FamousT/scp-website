Energy

Energy — Hydrocarbon and Renewable

The energy sector is in a period of transformation, and our Energy practice is designed to assist clients through this shifting landscape with informed, strategic legal guidance.

We support clients in traditional energy markets as well as in the renewable energy arena, understanding the unique regulatory, financial, and technological challenges that they face. Our services are comprehensive, covering project development, finance, acquisition, and regulation. We aim to help our clients achieve their objectives while advancing the broader move toward sustainable energy solutions.

Our team is not only familiar with the legal requirements of energy projects but also the commercial and environmental implications of these ventures. We assist in negotiating power purchase agreements, navigating complex permitting processes, and advising on energy-related M&A transactions. Our approach is tailored to the specific needs and circumstances of each client, ensuring that they can confidently move forward in an industry characterised by rapid change and innovation.

As the world pivots towards sustainable energy and carbon-neutral solutions, the legal landscape of the renewable energy sector grows increasingly complex. We advise developers, investors, lenders and others involved in this industry. From renewable energy project developers to investors and lenders, we offer comprehensive guidance that covers the intricate aspects of project finance, regulatory compliance, and intellectual property protection.

We advise on the legal aspects of renewable energy projects, including project development, environmental law, land use, and more. We understand the value of integrating emerging technologies like AI and the Internet of Things into the renewable energy sector, and we stand ready to counsel our clients on capitalising on these innovations while safeguarding their interests. By merging our legal expertise with an in-depth understanding of the renewable energy market, we help our clients not only to navigate the present landscape but also to shape it.

What we do

  • PIA compliance, licence conversion and host-community trusts
  • Gas sales, aggregation and midstream infrastructure agreements
  • Power purchase agreements and embedded generation
  • Renewable project development, land and grid access
  • Project finance security packages and lender diligence
