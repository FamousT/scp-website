Energy

Energy — Hydrocarbon and Renewable

Upstream, midstream, power and the transition in between.

We advise sponsors, lenders and offtakers across the Nigerian energy sector — from marginal field development and gas commercialisation under the Petroleum Industry Act, to embedded generation, mini-grids and utility-scale renewables.

Our energy work is transaction-led but regulator-facing. Licences, host-community agreements and ministerial consents usually determine whether a project reaches financial close, so we run those workstreams in parallel with the deal rather than behind it.

What we do

  • PIA compliance, licence conversion and host-community trusts
  • Gas sales, aggregation and midstream infrastructure agreements
  • Power purchase agreements and embedded generation
  • Renewable project development, land and grid access
  • Project finance security packages and lender diligence
