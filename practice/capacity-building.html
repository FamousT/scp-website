Public Governance & Fiscal Advisory

Capacity Building

Training and institutional development for public bodies and organisations.

Our Capacity Building practice helps government agencies, regulators and organisations build the knowledge and systems they need to carry out their mandates. We design and deliver training and support institutional reform.

Our programmes draw on our lawyers’ experience of tax administration, procurement, public finance and regulation, and are shaped around each institution’s own laws, processes and goals.

What we do

  • Training programmes for public institutions and regulators
  • Institutional and governance reviews
  • Procurement and public finance advisory
  • Manuals, guidelines and standard operating procedures
  • Support for revenue and regulatory agencies
