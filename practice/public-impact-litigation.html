Litigation

Dispute Resolution / Public Impact Litigation

Constitutional, regulatory and public-interest advocacy.

We litigate matters where the outcome reaches beyond the parties — constitutional challenges, judicial review of regulatory action, and cases that settle how a statute will be read for everyone who comes after.

The practice combines appellate advocacy with the policy work that surrounds it: submissions, coalition-building and engagement with the institutions whose conduct is under challenge.

What we do

  • Constitutional and fundamental-rights actions
  • Judicial review of regulatory and administrative decisions
  • Appellate advocacy to the Court of Appeal and Supreme Court
  • Class and representative proceedings
  • Amicus submissions and law-reform advocacy
