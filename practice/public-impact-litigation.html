Litigation

Dispute Resolution / Public Impact Litigation

Litigation can be disruptive to businesses and individuals alike, and our approach is to streamline the process to achieve favourable outcomes efficiently.

Our Commercial and Civil Litigation team provides skilled representation to clients facing legal disputes. We understand that litigation can be disruptive to businesses and individuals alike, and our approach is to streamline the process to achieve favourable outcomes efficiently.

With a deep understanding of courtroom procedures, legal strategy, and dispute resolution techniques, our team is poised to advocate for clients’ interests effectively. Our litigators have a proven track record in trials, appeals, and negotiations across a broad spectrum of commercial and civil issues.

We represent clients in a variety of disputes, including those involving contracts, business torts, property rights, and liability claims. Our strategy is to prepare meticulously for the possibility of trial while remaining open to settlement opportunities that serve our clients’ best interests. We pride ourselves on offering clear communication, robust representation, and strategic planning to navigate the complexities of litigation and protect our clients’ rights.

In an era where swift and amicable resolutions are preferred over protracted litigation, our Alternative Dispute Resolution practice offers a path to reconciling disputes efficiently. Our experienced lawyers employ mediation, arbitration, and collaborative law practices to resolve conflicts in a manner that is constructive and sustainable, tailoring our ADR strategies to the needs of our clients and facilitating discussions that lead to mutually acceptable solutions.

Our Special Tribunal practice is adept at guiding clients through the intricacies of specialised legal forums, including the Competition and Consumer Protection Tribunal, the Tax Appeal Tribunal and the Investment & Securities Tribunal. These forums often differ markedly from traditional courts in their procedural norms and substantive law application, and we provide meticulous preparation and adept handling of the rules and regulations unique to each.

What we do

  • Constitutional and fundamental-rights actions
  • Judicial review of regulatory and administrative decisions
  • Appellate advocacy to the Court of Appeal and Supreme Court
  • Class and representative proceedings
  • Amicus submissions and law-reform advocacy
