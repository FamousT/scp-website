Tax

Tax Advisory

In the web of taxation, businesses and individuals seek clarity and strategy to manage their obligations and seize tax efficiencies.

We advise businesses and individuals on managing their tax obligations in Nigeria and on the reliefs and efficiencies the law allows.

With a team that includes Fellows of the Chartered Institute of Taxation, we act on both advisory and contentious tax matters. Our representation before tax authorities and tribunals is marked by a strategic approach aimed at efficient and favourable resolutions.

We also run workshops and training on changes in tax law, so that clients can meet their tax obligations and plan for what is coming.

What we do

  • Transaction and holding-structure tax reviews
  • Transfer pricing documentation and defence
  • Tax health-checks and voluntary disclosure
  • FIRS and state revenue audits, objections and appeals
  • Incentive, free-zone and treaty-relief applications
