Tax

Tax Advisory

Structuring, controversy and cross-border tax positions.

We advise companies, funds and private clients on Nigerian tax exposure — corporate income tax, VAT, capital gains, transfer pricing and the treaty questions that arise when capital and people move across borders.

Most of our tax work is preventative. We review positions before they harden into assessments, and where a dispute is unavoidable we handle it through objection, the Tax Appeal Tribunal and beyond.

What we do

  • Transaction and holding-structure tax reviews
  • Transfer pricing documentation and defence
  • Tax health-checks and voluntary disclosure
  • FIRS and state revenue audits, objections and appeals
  • Incentive, free-zone and treaty-relief applications
