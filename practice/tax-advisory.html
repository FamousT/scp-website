Tax Advisory

In the web of taxation, businesses and individuals seek clarity and strategy to manage their obligations and seize tax efficiencies.

In the web of taxation, businesses and individuals seek clarity and strategy to manage their obligations and seize tax efficiencies. Our Taxation practice stands as a beacon of expertise in Nigeria’s complex tax environment.

With a team that includes Fellows of the Chartered Institute of Taxation, we offer a wealth of knowledge and experience in both advisory and contentious tax matters. Our representation before tax authorities and tribunals is marked by a strategic approach aimed at efficient and favourable resolutions.

Furthermore, we recognise the importance of staying ahead of the curve, offering workshops and training to keep our clients informed and prepared for the ever-evolving tax landscape. Our commitment to delivering practical and effective tax solutions ensures that our clients are well-positioned to fulfill their tax responsibilities while optimising their financial benefits.

What we do

  • Transaction and holding-structure tax reviews
  • Transfer pricing documentation and defence
  • Tax health-checks and voluntary disclosure
  • FIRS and state revenue audits, objections and appeals
  • Incentive, free-zone and treaty-relief applications
