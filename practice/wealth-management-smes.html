Wealth Management & SME Advisory

Wealth Management and SMEs

In today’s world, managing personal wealth, liabilities, and assets has become increasingly complex.

In today’s world, managing personal wealth, liabilities, and assets has become increasingly complex. We advise on personal asset management and estate planning.

Tailored to the individual needs of our clients, our Wealth Management and Estate Planning services encompass a wide range of offerings, from investment management to the establishment and administration of wills and trusts. We specialise in constructing precise strategies for asset accumulation and preservation, mindful of the unique objectives of each client.

Our firm advises on the crafting of business succession plans, taking into consideration the complex implications of tax, management continuity, and ownership transition. We are also versed in planning for eventualities such as retirement, incapacity, and provisions for minors, ensuring that our clients’ legacies are secured, and their intentions are fully realised. Much of this work is for high-net-worth individuals (HNIs), whom we advise on investment decisions and on wealth and estate management.

In the world of SMEs and aggregated service entities, we understand that each enterprise, while smaller in scale, requires a legal strategy that is just as rigorous as larger corporations. We serve small and medium-sized businesses by addressing entity formation, compliance, and contractual agreements. Our understanding of aggregated service models enables us to facilitate cooperative ventures and partnerships that allow our clients to achieve economies of scale.

We provide guidance on risk management, employment law, and intellectual property protection, ensuring clients are compliant but also strategically positioned to capitalise on market opportunities. For aggregated service businesses specifically, we offer strategic advice on forming and operating collective entities, managing joint contracts, and navigating shared compliance obligations, delivering practical, actionable legal counsel that propels their business forward.

What we do

  • Trusts, wills, estate administration and probate
  • Family holding structures and succession governance
  • SME retainers and outsourced general counsel
  • Shareholder agreements and founder arrangements
  • Seed and growth fundraising documentation
