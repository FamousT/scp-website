Practice

Capital Formation & Capital Raise

Counsel for companies, sponsors and investors raising and deploying capital in Nigeria and across the wider African market.

Businesses and public-sector entities raise capital through private equity, debt finance or the capital markets. The right route depends on the issuer’s stage, its control objectives, the cost of capital and the approvals required.

We advise companies, investors, lenders, trustees and public-sector issuers on each of these routes. The firm has acted on DFI-backed financings, acquisition financing for a listed energy group, and bond programmes for several State Governments.

We work alongside the firm’s tax, M&A and dispute resolution teams, so structuring, approvals and enforcement risk are handled within one mandate.

What we do

  • Capital-raise readiness reviews and share-capital restructuring
  • Private equity, venture and growth investments
  • Private placements, rights issues and recapitalisations
  • Corporate, acquisition and DFI financings, and security packages
  • Bond, sukuk and debt issuances, and SEC and NGX approvals
