Consumer Credit

Consumer Credit and Digital Money Lending

Licensing and conduct standards for lenders and credit platforms.

Digital lending in Nigeria is now a licensed, supervised activity with real conduct obligations. We take lenders through FCCPC registration and CBN licensing, and we build the disclosure, collection and data practices that keep an approval in place once it is granted.

We act for banks, fintechs, buy-now-pay-later platforms and credit bureaux, and we advise investors on what a lender's regulatory posture is actually worth in diligence.

What we do

  • FCCPC digital money lender registration and renewal
  • CBN licensing for finance companies and payment providers
  • Loan documentation, disclosure and pricing review
  • Debt-collection conduct standards and complaint handling
  • Borrower data, credit reporting and consent frameworks
