Biography

As Accountant at SimmonsCooper Partners, Temitope Omotayo plays an important role in the firm's success and operational efficiency through meticulous financial planning and auditing.

He is a qualified accountant with a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Lagos State University and an MBA from the same institution. Temitope joined SimmonsCooper Partners in 2011 as an Executive Assistant and rose through the ranks to his current position. He is a vital part of the team and exemplifies the firm's value of excellence in his work output.

A commercial law firm providing holistic legal support for the Nigerian market and beyond, from offices in Lagos and Abuja.
