Energy

Financing structure for a multi-asset gas-to-power project

What it was
A sponsor consortium developing a multi-asset gas-to-power project across three states needed a financing structure that satisfied international lenders while surviving Nigerian regulatory review.
Why it mattered
Gas-to-power projects fail at the interface between the security package lenders want and the consents the state will grant. Getting that interface right determined whether the project reached financial close at all.
Our role
We advised the sponsors on the security package, host-community development trust, regulatory consents across three states, and the inter-creditor arrangements. We ran the consent workstream in parallel with the financing so approvals landed before the long-stop date.

Dispute Resolution / Public Impact Litigation

Defence of a multinational in a landmark commercial dispute

Outcome

Claim dismissed

What it was
A multinational faced a claim that threatened both its operating licence and its position in the Nigerian market.
Why it mattered
The case turned on a point of statutory interpretation that had not been settled at appellate level. The outcome set the reading for every operator in the sector.
Our role
We led a five-year defence through trial and appeal to a full dismissal at appellate level, preserving the client's licence and market position, and establishing the interpretation now relied on across the sector.

Tech & AI

Licensing pathway for a pan-African payments platform

Outcome

First-of-its-kind

What it was
A payments platform operating across several African markets needed a Nigerian entry structure that would not compromise its existing licences elsewhere.
Why it mattered
No comparable structure had been approved. The engagement required the regulator to accept a model it had not seen before.
Our role
We structured the entity, built the data-protection posture, and led the CBN engagement strategy through to approval — the first authorisation of its kind for a cross-border platform of this design.
