Careers

Train here. Then argue anywhere.

We hire lawyers who want responsibility early and are willing to be tested on it. Our training programme is structured, our feedback is direct, and our juniors appear in real matters from the first year.

Training Programme

Two years, four practices, one named mentor.

Our trainee programme runs over two years with four six-month rotations, chosen with you rather than assigned to you. Every trainee is paired with a partner mentor who is accountable for their development, not just their workload.

Formal training runs alongside the rotations: advocacy workshops, drafting clinics, regulatory practice sessions and a structured reading programme. Trainees attend client meetings and regulator engagements from the first rotation.

What we look for

  • Clear written reasoning — we test this before anything else
  • Commercial curiosity: why the client wants what they are asking for
  • Willingness to be corrected, and to correct others
  • Genuine interest in at least one regulated sector
