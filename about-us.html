Who We Are

Clear, practical counsel for business in Nigeria and Africa.

SimmonsCooper Partners is a Nigerian law firm founded in 2006. We are based in Lagos, Nigeria’s commercial centre, with an office in Abuja, where we work closely with regulators and government institutions.

We advise large corporations, government bodies, growing businesses and individuals. Whatever the size of your firm, you get clear, practical guidance from a team focused on your result. Our lawyers have also contributed to the laws and regulatory frameworks that shape business in Nigeria today.

How We Work

Four commitments on every matter.

How we staff, run and resolve your matter, wherever it sits in the firm.

01

Partner-led teams

Every matter is led by a partner, so you work with senior lawyers who know your matter from start to finish.

02

One team for every matter

If your matter crosses practice areas, you are not passed around. We bring the right lawyers together into one team. Our transaction and dispute lawyers work side by side, so your contracts are drafted to prevent disputes. If one does arise, the team handling it already understands the deal behind it.

03

Skilled in resolving disputes

We handle commercial litigation, arbitration and mediation, including complex, high-value disputes. The firm’s lawyers have acted in arbitrations under the ICC and LCIA Rules. Several of the firm’s lawyers hold relevant arbitration qualifications.

04

Focused on business and regulation in Nigeria and Africa

Our work centres on commercial and regulatory matters in Nigeria and across Africa. We are the only Nigerian member of Globalaw, a network of over 100 independent law firms worldwide. Wherever your business, assets or disputes are located, we have trusted partners ready to help.
