Data Protection Compliance

A Nigeria Data Protection Act compliance programme — assessment, documentation, and the annual audit filing.

Service level

4–8 weeks to programme handover

Engagement

Fixed fee, with an optional annual retainer

What this covers

  • Data mapping and record of processing activities
  • Privacy notices, consent flows and internal policies
  • Data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing
  • Processor and cross-border transfer agreements
  • Breach response plan and staff training pack
  • Support with the annual NDPC audit filing

Who it is for

Any business processing personal data at scale — platforms, lenders, health, education and HR-heavy employers.
