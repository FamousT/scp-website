Press & Media

Media enquiries.

Journalists and researchers should use the form opposite, selecting Press / media as the topic. Requests reach the Marketing team, who will arrange comment, background or an interview with the relevant partner.

We aim to respond to media enquiries the same working day. For deadlines inside two hours, note the deadline in the message and call the Lagos switchboard after submitting.

What we can usually provide

  • Named comment on regulatory and legislative developments
  • Background briefing on sector regulation, on an unattributed basis
  • Partner biographies and high-resolution portraits
  • Firm boilerplate and logo assets
