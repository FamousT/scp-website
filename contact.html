Press & Media

Media enquiries.

Journalists and researchers should use the enquiry form above and say in the message that the request is from the media. We will arrange comment, background or an interview with the relevant partner.

We aim to respond to media enquiries the same working day. For deadlines inside two hours, note the deadline in your message and call the Lagos switchboard after submitting.

For journalists

What we can usually provide

  • Named comment on regulatory and legislative developments
  • Background briefing on sector regulation, on an unattributed basis
  • Partner biographies and high-resolution portraits
  • Firm boilerplate and logo assets

Start your message with “Media request” and include your deadline.
