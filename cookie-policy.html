Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in much the same way. In this policy, “cookies” covers both.

2. Your choice comes first

When you first visit this website, a banner asks whether you accept or reject non-essential cookies. Until you choose, no analytics runs. The office maps on our contact page are provided by Google Maps and load with that page; Google may set its own cookies when they do. Rejecting them does not stop you using the website. You can change your choice at any time through , which also appears at the foot of every page. This approach follows the consent requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

4. Withdrawing consent

If you accepted analytics cookies and later reject them through Cookie settings, analytics stops at once and we remove the analytics cookies we can reach. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-party cookies

Google sets and controls its own cookies under its own privacy policy. We have no access to them. They are set only if you agree.
