Public Governance & Tax Advisory Services

Resolution of Multi-Year Personal Income Tax Dispute for a High-Net-Worth Individual

Outcome
Substantial reduction of the client’s assessed tax exposure and resolution of liabilities spanning approximately seven assessment years on terms satisfactory to the client.
What it was
We represented a high-net-worth individual in connection with disputed personal income tax assessments issued by the Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS), involving alleged liabilities running into hundreds of millions of naira and spanning approximately seven assessment years.
Why it mattered
The engagement involved significant historical tax exposure across multiple assessment periods and required a detailed reconciliation of the client’s actual tax position against the assessments raised by the tax authority. Through the engagement, the client’s assessed exposure was reduced from hundreds of millions of naira to a single-digit million-naira liability. The resolution also regularised the client’s tax position and provided certainty regarding his outstanding tax obligations.
Our role
We reviewed the assessments and underlying tax position, developed the basis for challenging and reconciling the disputed liabilities, engaged extensively with the LIRS on the client’s behalf, and negotiated a substantial reduction of the assessed exposure to an agreed and satisfactory liability. The outcome strengthened the client’s confidence in our tax practice and led to our continued engagement on subsequent tax matters.

Corporate Commercial & General Transactions

Writing the Rulebook for Nigerian Competition Law

Outcome
More than 15 regulatory instruments drafted for the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), which set how mergers are reviewed and how market conduct is assessed in Nigeria.
What it was
The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 gave Nigeria a competition statute but left the detail to subsidiary rules. Businesses and the Commission needed those rules to know how mergers are notified and reviewed, how dominance is assessed, and how a cartel member can come forward under leniency. We drafted them first as consultants to the World Bank and now directly for the FCCPC.
Why it mattered
These rules shape how transactions are reviewed, how cartels and other restrictive agreements are treated, when authorizations may be granted, and how dominance is assessed in Nigeria. With little local precedent to draw on, each instrument had to adapt international practice to the realities of the Nigerian market.
Our role
We wrote the rules: which deals get reviewed, when market power becomes abused, and how cartel members can come forward. Then we trained the officers who enforced them. Today, we advise clients under the same framework.

Corporate Commercial & General Transactions

Structuring an Investment Exit from the Upstream Petroleum Sector

Outcome
A negotiated restructuring that enabled African Capital Alliance investee entities to exit their equity position in a Nigerian upstream oil and gas company.
What it was
African Capital Alliance investee entities held a significant stake in the company and sought to exit the upstream petroleum business. The interlinked equity and debt positions made the route to exit complex.
Why it mattered
A conventional share sale would not have addressed the full position. The exit required ownership, shareholder and mezzanine debt, financing arrangements and regulatory requirements to be addressed together within a single, coordinated transaction.
Our role
We developed the restructuring strategy, evaluated alternative exit structures, and prepared and negotiated transaction documentation. We managed the corporate approvals, engaged the petroleum and competition regulators, and advised on tax treatment, aligning each step with the implementation of the exit.
